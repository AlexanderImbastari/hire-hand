# Offer Catalog

What we charge for, and what is deliberately free.

## Contractor Pro — $20/month

The only paid product. No tiers, no annual plan at launch.

**Free (approved contractor, no subscription)**
- Browse all open jobs, full detail and photos
- Public profile
- Service area filtering
- 3 lifetime quotes

**Paid**
- Unlimited quotes
- Everything above

Nothing else is gated.

## Why the gate is on quoting, not contact info

Browsing is free because it solves the cold-start problem. A contractor
who signs up and sees twelve real jobs in their zip will pay. One who
hits a wall won't.

The 3 free quotes exist so a contractor can win a job before we charge.
One won job pays for years of subscription, which makes the $20 an easy
yes instead of a gamble.

Contact details and exact address unlock when a homeowner **accepts** a
quote — for free and paid contractors alike. That is a workflow step,
not a paywall. Gating contact info would be a lead-gate wearing a
subscription costume, it leaks anyway (contractors type their number into
the quote text), and it undercuts the entire reason to choose us over
Thumbtack.

Showing approximate location publicly and exact address only after
acceptance is a homeowner safety rule, not a monetization lever. Don't
move it.

## Homeowners

Free. Always. No posting fee, no booking fee, no commission at launch.

## Billing rules

- Only an `approved` contractor can start a subscription.
- On `past_due`, quoting is blocked but existing pending quotes stay live
  and the profile stays visible.
- On cancellation, access continues to the end of the paid period.
- The 3 free quotes are lifetime, not monthly, and do not reset on
  cancellation.

## Not building yet

Featured placement, lead credits, annual billing, homeowner-side paid
tiers, commission on completed jobs. Do not add billing surface area
without asking.
